Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FUTURERECEİPT handles information when you use our digital receipt and spending insight platform. FUTURERECEİPT is currently provided as an Open Beta project for testing and educational purposes.

What Data We Collect

Depending on how you use FUTURERECEİPT, we may collect:

  • Account information: your email address, name, and account role (customer or merchant).
  • Purchase history: digital receipt details such as merchant name, transaction date, items, prices, totals, tax, payment method details supplied by a merchant, and related receipt information.
  • Preferences and usage information: settings and choices you make in the app, such as spending and notification preferences.

We do not ask for or store full payment card numbers, CVVs, PINs, bank account numbers, or other sensitive payment credentials.

How We Use Data

We use the information above only to operate and improve the FUTURERECEİPT experience, including to:

  • create, verify, deliver, and display digital receipts;
  • show your receipt history and provide spending analysis;
  • calculate informational environmental impact estimates; and
  • secure accounts, prevent misuse, and maintain the service.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your receipt history for third-party advertising. Service providers that help us host, authenticate, or operate FUTURERECEİPT may process information only as needed to provide those services and under appropriate instructions.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, may give you the following rights, subject to applicable exceptions:

  • Right to know: request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use, or disclose.
  • Right to delete: request that we permanently delete your personal information from our systems. FUTURERECEİPT provides account deletion controls in Profile Settings, and California residents may use those controls to request deletion of their account and associated data.
  • Right to correct: request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: FUTURERECEİPT does not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to non-discrimination: you will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising a privacy right.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We will not require you to create an account to submit a privacy request, except where reasonably necessary to verify the request. You may submit a deletion request through Profile Settings when signed in.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve issues, and comply with applicable requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as FUTURERECEİPT changes during the Open Beta. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Important: FUTURERECEİPT does not process payments or provide financial advice. Receipt and environmental information is provided for informational purposes.
