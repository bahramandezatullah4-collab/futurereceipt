Privacy Policy

Open Beta

Who we are and contact

FUTURERECEİPT is an open-beta digital receipt service. For privacy questions or requests, email futurereceiptfeedback@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may process account and profile details such as your name, email address, phone number, account type, preferences, and business or store information. We also process receipt details, including merchant, address, date, items, quantities, amounts, taxes, verification codes, and payment or transaction details supplied by a merchant. If the app offers feedback, survey, or screenshot features, we process what you choose to submit.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, store and display receipts, provide spending summaries and environmental estimates, respond to requests, and test and improve the beta.

Payments and card data

FUTURERECEİPT does not process payments or move money. We do not request or store full card numbers, CVV/CVC codes, PINs, or payment credentials. A receipt may include limited details supplied by a merchant, such as payment method, masked card details, or transaction and terminal identifiers.

Service providers and sharing

Clerk provides authentication and account management. Replit provides hosting, database, and file-storage services used to operate the platform. Receipt details may be available to the customer and merchant associated with that transaction. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

Clerk may use cookies or similar storage to maintain secure sign-in sessions. The app may save preferences such as language and sign-up options needed to complete registration in your browser’s local storage. The current application code does not include advertising pixels.

Retention and deletion

We keep information while it is needed to provide and protect the service. You can update profile information or request account deletion from the app. Deleting an account removes the profile and sign-in account; some receipts may remain without a direct customer link or in merchant history so other parties’ records remain available.

Your choices and security

You can review or change information available in Profile Settings and request help or deletion at futurereceiptfeedback@gmail.com. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Minors and changes

Use the platform only if you are old enough to create an account under the laws that apply where you live. This policy may be updated while the product is in beta; the current version will be posted on this page.
